Student Assistant Chatbot Details

Personal Project: AI-Powered Academic Assistant

The Student Assistant Chatbot is an intelligent conversational agent developed to modernize university support. Using Python and Natural Language Processing (NLP), I built a system that provides students with 24/7 access to academic resources, administrative FAQs, and personalized guidance.

1. NLP & Intent Recognition Engine

The core of the chatbot is a custom NLP pipeline built with libraries like NLTK and spaCy. I implemented a sophisticated Intent Recognition system that allows the bot to understand the "why" behind a student's query, even when using slang or natural variations in phrasing. Key technical steps included:

  • Tokenization & Lemmatization: Breaking down sentences into root words to reduce noise and improve matching accuracy.
  • Entity Extraction: Identifying specific data points such as "Course Codes," "Professor Names," or "Due Dates" directly from the user's message.
  • Context Management: Developing logic that remembers previous parts of the conversation to handle multi-turn dialogues effectively.

2. Information Architecture & Integration

I designed a Knowledge Base that the chatbot queries in real-time. This allows it to categorize and resolve student issues in the following areas:

  • Academic Support: Instant retrieval of course prerequisites and study guide locations.
  • Administrative Automation: Handling routine questions about enrollment deadlines, campus facilities, and office hours.
  • Sentiment Analysis: I integrated basic sentiment scoring to detect frustrated users and prioritize their inquiries for human handoff.

Impact & Performance

By automating over 70% of routine inquiries, the chatbot significantly reduced the administrative workload on faculty while ensuring students received immediate, accurate assistance at any time of day.
